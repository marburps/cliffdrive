🏁 X-LAN Racing — 2 Players

Split-screen race: P1 on top, P2 on bottom, same circuit, same oncoming traffic.

🔴 P1 (top): first gamepad (L stick steer, R2 gas, L2 brake, R stick ↑↓ shift)  or  ⌨️ W A S D + E/Q

🔵 P2 (bottom): second gamepad (same buttons)  or  ⌨️ Arrow Keys

🚦 One start light — the first START/Enter sets both cars on the grid and starts the sequence.

⚠️ Off-road, head-on (+30%) and car-vs-car contact (+10% each) deal damage. 100% damage = wrecked, the other player keeps racing.

🔧 3 laps each. You start in 1st — shift up to go faster. 6 gears. Don't leave it in 1st forever!

Press START (either controller) or tap Enter to begin
